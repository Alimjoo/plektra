Plektra is a free, open-source Android input method for phones with physical keyboards.

It provides configurable hardware layouts, Shift/Ctrl/Alt/SYM behaviour, navigation shortcuts, suggestions, snippets, emoji and symbol pages, clipboard history, themes, backup and restore, and a compact Slim mode.

Plektra is developed and release-tested on the Unihertz Titan 2 and Titan 2 Elite. Stable and Nightly use separate app identities and can be installed together.
